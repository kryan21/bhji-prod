About Us

Frame your emotions

Weddings take months to plan and pass in the blink of an eye. Our job — our joy — is to make sure not one real moment of yours is ever lost.

Our Story

A Delhi team with one obsession

Bhaiya Ji Photobale is a creative team of photographers and cinematic filmmakers based in Delhi. Our promise is in our bio, and we mean every word: our creative team captures every lovely moment of your wedding.

Not just the stage moments — the lovely ones. Your mother adjusting your dupatta. Your father pretending he is not emotional. The haldi thrown a second before the camera was ready. We live for those frames.

From Delhi ballrooms to a temple wedding at Triyoginarayan and pre-weddings in Manali, we bring the same calm crew, cinema-grade Sony Alpha kit, FPV drones and instant reels — and we cover both families like they are our own. Because by the end of your wedding, they usually feel like it.
